Built in 3D before it's built for real

Every purchased part is placed at its real size. The model plays the assembly in 27 steps — with the bolts, checks and purchase link for each part — and animates the drop-off sequence.

The model's interface is in Korean. It is a layout and assembly study, not a manufacturing drawing: strength, friction and motor torque have not been calculated yet, and the 0.5 kg payload is a test target, not a rating.

Design

Four subsystems, one chassis

A 264 × 520 mm two-deck chassis (348 mm across the wheels) carries a cargo box, a drop-off module and an elevator-button mast.

01 · Drive & power

Four-wheel drive on a LiFePO4 pack

  • Four JGB37-520 encoder motors on Ø85 mm wheels, driven by a Cytron MDD10A.
  • 12.8 V 9.9 Ah LiFePO4 pack with fused power branches.
  • An emergency stop and relay cut motor power independently of the computers.
02 · Drop-off module

Conveyor + two-stage folding ramp

  • A 250 mm green PVC belt (900 mm loop) driven by a 27 rpm worm-gear motor (~52 mm/s), top surface 219 mm above the floor.
  • A 250 + 250 mm ramp unfolds on two 12 rpm worm-gear axes to ~24°; a 100 N gas spring offsets its weight and a servo-released plunger locks it when stowed.
  • Limit switches and IR break-beams confirm the ramp position and that the package has fully left.
03 · Elevator button

A mast that presses lift buttons

  • A 2040 aluminum column puts the pusher tip at 950 mm.
  • A DS3218 servo drives a pushrod along a linear bushing; a compression spring limits the pressing force (stroke ≈ 34 mm).
04 · Sensing & safety

See the path, watch the drop zone

  • Pi Camera 3 and five VL53L1X ToF sensors at the front and sides; two more watch the floor behind for the drop-off.
  • A 130° USB camera with an LED strip photographs the delivered package by the door.
  • An INA226 current monitor stops a motor that stalls (2.5 A for 0.5–1 s).

Software · planned

Ordering to delivery

Orders come in through a web app; the robot navigates the school by ArUco checkpoints, a rule engine adapts its behavior in real time, PPO reinforcement learning handles obstacle avoidance and routing, and a FastAPI dashboard oversees it live.
