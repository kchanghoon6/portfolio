Experiments

Three test beds, one method

Each stage answered a limitation of the one before and was submitted to a later round of the fair. The figures are the original poster panels — not redrawn.

v1 · Round 1 · June 2026

Foam-board model bridge

  • Proof that the whole pipeline — collect, compensate, detect, explain — runs on a ~₩190k node.
  • Compensation cut false alarms in the humidity segment 17.5% → 9.1%.
  • Under simulated damage, threshold exceedance rose 4.5% → 29.7%.
Full-scale data · openLAB, TU Dresden

A 45 m research bridge, public data

  • The autoencoder alone stayed at 0.1× its threshold while the frequency rose 6–8% in October — blind to the season.
  • With a frequency channel, autumn false alarms fell 26% → 0% after temperature compensation.
v2 · Round 2 · July 2026

Aluminum model bridge, 27 conditions

  • Low-damping aluminum and a solenoid auto-tapper fixed the two problems of v1: blurred peaks and uneven manual taps.
  • All 840 structural-change windows detected (recall 100%); false alarms 4/90 (4.4%); ROC-AUC 0.996.
  • Band contributions tell bolt loosening (40–80 Hz ratio, 76–90%) from added mass (amplitude/energy, 73–80%).
v3 · Round 3 · September 2026

Two real footbridges

  • Five overpasses screened by 30-minute ambient recordings; E (1.74 Hz) and C (3.81 Hz) chosen for their sharp peaks.
  • Bridge E, Paju: the 1.74 Hz peak held within 1% for 68 h, with no consecutive-window alarms.
  • Bridge C, Changwon: a 200 kg load dropped the frequency 0.6% and it recovered on removal — the added-mass prediction; alarms fired only while loaded.
